The Blue Umbrella

The Blue Umbrella

By Ruskin Bond

About the book: Set in a Himalayan village, the story centres on Binya, a young girl who owns a beautiful blue umbrella. The umbrella attracts attention and stirs envy, leading to a conflict that gradually gives way to kindness.

Reflection: The story explores generosity, contentment and the value of human relationships. Its simple setting and gentle storytelling invite readers to think about how small acts of kindness can change the way people treat one another.

Themes: Kindness · Envy · Generosity
The Little Prince

The Little Prince

By Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

About the book: A pilot stranded in the desert meets a young prince from another planet. Through the prince's travels and encounters, the story considers how people see the world and form connections.

Reflection: Beneath its fable-like simplicity, the book raises thoughtful questions about friendship, responsibility and what matters in life. It can be read as an imaginative adventure or as a story with deeper meanings.

Themes: Friendship · Love · Responsibility
Malgudi Days

Malgudi Days

By R. K. Narayan

About the book: This collection of short stories is set in the fictional South Indian town of Malgudi. Its characters include students, workers, families and people from many walks of life, each facing familiar everyday situations.

Reflection: Narayan uses humour and close observation to portray ordinary experiences. The stories offer a glimpse of community life and show how small events can reveal much about human nature.

Themes: Everyday life · Society · Humour
The Room on the Roof

The Room on the Roof

By Ruskin Bond

About the book: The novel follows Rusty, an anglo-Indian teenager living in Dehradun, as he moves beyond a restrictive home life and forms friendships while trying to find his place in the world.

Reflection: The book explores growing up, independence and belonging. Rusty's experiences show that freedom also brings uncertainty, and that friendship and understanding can help people through change.

Themes: Growing up · Freedom · Belonging
The 3 Mistakes of My Life

The 3 Mistakes of My Life

By Chetan Bhagat

About the book: Set in Ahmedabad, the novel follows friends Govind, Ishaan and Omi as they pursue a business idea connected to cricket. Their plans are affected by personal choices and events in the wider community.

Reflection: The story brings together friendship, ambition, sport and difficult decisions. Its contemporary setting makes it accessible to readers interested in how personal goals can intersect with family and social expectations.

Themes: Friendship · Ambition · Choices

About this project

A place to read and reflect

The Book Nook is a student-created book review blog. It brings together short introductions and reflections on five books, with the aim of encouraging reading, clear writing and thoughtful discussion. The reviews are starting points for personal responses from the reader.
